# Working with Giulia on Lifework

## How we work

**One step at a time.** Giulia sketched the whole project up front. That sketch is
context, not a backlog to work through. Do the one step she asked for, deliver it,
stop, and wait. Do not prepare the next step, do not "while I'm here" anything.

**Ask before doing something she has not asked for.** If a thing seems obviously
useful and she has not asked for it, that is exactly the moment to ask. She will
say yes or no. Guessing costs her tokens and puts things in her systems she did
not agree to. Her words: "You think you know better, but you don't."

**Do not infer rules and then act on them.** If a rule is not something she said,
it is not a rule. State inferences as questions.

**Do not improve her data.** Her spellings, her names, her wording. If another
source disagrees, she is right. Bring the disagreement to her; do not resolve it.

## Data rules

- **Notion is the source of truth, and read-only.** Never write to Notion. Never
  change anything in Notion. Right now the job is one-way: copy Notion into the
  new environment. A write-back may come later, only when she asks for it.
- **Upwork is out of scope.** Do not read from it, do not merge it in, do not use
  it to correct or enrich anything. "That's for later."
- Client information belongs in the client zone, not scattered through the rest
  of the environment.
- No client data in the repo. It stays in Supabase and in Notion.

## Delivery

- SQL goes in the chat as a copy-paste block. Not a file, not a download, not an
  artifact. One step per message, nothing else in that message.
- Supabase migrations must be safe to re-run.
